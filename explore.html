📜 History of Manga

Manga has existed in Japan for centuries, but modern manga became popular after World War II thanks to legendary artist Osamu Tezuka, often called the "God of Manga." Today, manga is read by millions of people around the world.

➡ How to Read Manga

Unlike most Western comics, Japanese manga is usually read from right to left. Readers start from the back cover and move toward the front while reading each page from right to left.

Manga vs Manhwa vs Manhua

🇯🇵 Manga = Japanese comics
🇰🇷 Manhwa = Korean comics
🇨🇳 Manhua = Chinese comics

🌍 Manga Around the World

Manga has become a global phenomenon. Countries like the United States, India, France, South Korea, Brazil, and many others have millions of manga fans. Many popular anime are adapted from successful manga series.
